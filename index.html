About Croesus

Incorporated in the jurisdiction of Mauritius, Croesus Treasury Management Ltd is regulated and licensed by the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius. The Company crafts bespoke solutions to assist clients to take informed market decisions and achieve their execution objectives.

Liquidity management solutions, designed to optimise otherwise unused excess cash and liquidity to derive revenue, are an integral part of this fintech firm’s endeavour towards a holistic perspective to treasury management. Because every client is different, realities and constraints are carefully studied in order to devise customised solutions.

Our services

The Company provides treasury services to clients that do not have an in-house expertise and infrastructure. The services provided will be specifically focussed on currency management.

Currency management

Treasury services for clients without in-house expertise and infrastructure, specifically focussed on currency management.

Currency Overlay

To effectively minimise risks with regard to open positions or future exposure on the market that may require coverage.

Cash and Liquidity Management

To assist clients to derive a return from otherwise dormant cash. Bearing in mind the constraints of our clients, we devise tailor-made solutions to fit the specific needs of the client at hand.

Payment Processing

To process payments on behalf of clients which adds an independent layer of control over the handling of payments and more importantly allows clients to focus resources on core business activities.
